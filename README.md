## The game

Danny Reyes drove deliveries for a living — until the debts came due. Now fixer Marta Solano owns his evenings: four jobs across **Meridian Bay**, a golden-hour coastal city.

- **A real city**: a 7×7 block grid with named avenues and streets, lane markings, crosswalks, traffic lights, trees, street lamps, a waterfront promenade, a pier and working docks on the bay.
- **On foot and by car**: walk, run, punch, shoot (pistol, 12-round magazine, reload). Five stealable cars with distinct handling — the Meridian S sedan, the Bay Cab taxi, the Veloce GT coupe, the Rancher pickup and the Cargo Van.
- **Wanted level 1–5**: steal a car, hit a pedestrian, shoot at the police, and patrol cars chase, ram and try to bust you. Break line of sight and the heat fades; stand still in front of a patrol car and you get BUSTED.
- **Four story missions**: steal a car for the garage, run a package from the pier to the Harbor Hotel, lose the heat, then one last getaway to the dock warehouse — with timers and cash rewards.
- **A living world**: AI traffic that follows the road graph and stops at red lights, pedestrians who walk the sidewalks and flee gunfire, a hospital respawn with a fine, health that regenerates, a full HUD (cash, stars, health, objective + timer, speed, ammo, street name, game clock) and a live minimap.
- **Feel**: third-person and chase cameras, shadows, smoke, sparks, muzzle flash and screen shake, fully synthesized WebAudio (engine, sirens, gunshots, city ambience) — zero network requests.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: **W A S D** drive / walk, **Shift** run, **E** enter / exit a car, **Click** shoot, **F** punch, **R** reload, **Space** handbrake, **C** camera, **P** pause, **M** mute. Progress saves in the browser (cash, jobs done, best heat).
